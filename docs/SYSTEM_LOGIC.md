# ⚡ EPCS System Logic: National Security & Circular Materials

## 1. Primary Objectives (Core System Purpose)
* **National Critical Minerals Strategy:** Captures and extracts high-value Rare Earth Elements (REEs) and critical metals—specifically Scandium and Vanadium—directly from power-generation coal ash[cite: 2, 3].
* **Alkali Geocrete Synthesis:** Processes residual ash with alkali activation to form high-strength, structural Geocrete (6,000 PSI) for underground utility infrastructure[cite: 1, 2].
* **Coal Ash Hazard Mitigation:** Eliminates large, hazardous surface coal combustion residual (CCR) impoundments and ash storage ponds by converting 100% of the feedstock into commercial commodities[cite: 2, 3].

---

## 2. Infrastructure & Downstream Integration
* **Subterranean Utilidors:** Structural Geocrete forms a 4-pipe common trunk system housing isolated fluid loops with leak protection and EMP shielding[cite: 1].
* **Data Center Energy Sync ("Electronic Blood"):** Recovered Vanadium Pentoxide ($V_2O_5$) fuels Redox Flow Battery (VRFB) stacks to deliver direct DC power and thermal cooling to server racks[cite: 1, 3].
* **Electrochemical Voltage Translation:** Replaces bulky copper transformers with low-voltage stacked cells to match cabinet-level power requirements, reducing RF interference and facility heat loads[cite: 1].

---

## 3. Operational Logic & System Dynamics
* **Flow Determination:** System electrolyte flow rates dynamically scale to ensure cooling capacity exceeds electronic heat output:
  $$Flow = \max(Thermal\_Demand, Electrochemical\_Demand)$$[cite: 1]
* **Cell Scalability:** Cell surface area controls total current output ($A$), while cell stack count adjusts target operating voltage ($V$)[cite: 1].
* **Off-Gas Recovery:** Captures hydrogen and oxygen off-gassing at both charge and discharge terminals to recycle chemical energy and maximize system efficiency.
