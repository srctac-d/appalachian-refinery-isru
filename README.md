> **Patent Pending:** Provisional Application Filed July 4, 2025

## 🎯 Executive Summary
The Electrochemical Power and Cooling System (EPCS) unifies raw material recovery with data center energy infrastructure. By extracting high-purity Vanadium Pentoxide ($V_2O_5$) from 600 TPD of Coal Combustion Residuals (CCR), EPCS fuels a closed-loop "Electronic Blood" Vanadium Redox Flow Battery (VRFB) system that supplies direct DC power and liquid thermal management to high-density server racks.

## 🌟 Primary Innovations
1. **Electrochemical Voltage Translation:** Replaces traditional wired copper transformers with stacked redox cells supplying direct DC power to cabinets.
2. **Dual-Function Core:** Electrolyte acts simultaneously as the energy storage medium and primary coolant, eliminating standalone HVAC systems.

## 1. Technical Performance Specs
* **Transformer Replacement:** Replaces traditional wired copper transformers with stacked redox cells to eliminate heavy transformer losses and reduce RF interference.
* **Efficiency Pivot:** Bypasses AC/DC conversion losses by supplying stable DC power directly to electronics.
* **Thermal Management:** Electrolyte acts as the primary coolant, removing separate cooling infrastructure and lowering facility fire risk.

## 2. Infrastructure (The Utilidor)
* **Shell:** 6,000 PSI Geocrete derived from 600 TPD refinery mineral processing.
* **Circuitry:** 4-pipe common trunk system providing isolated liquid loops for primary supply and secondary return lines.
* **Safety:** Integrated leak-capture drains and subterranean EMP shielding.

## 3. Operational Logic & Dynamics
* **Flow Determination:** 
  $$\text{Flow Rate} = \max(\text{Thermal\_Demand}, \text{Electrochemical\_Demand})$$
* **Scalability:** Cell area controls current ($A$); cell count adjusts target voltage ($V$).
* **Electrolysis Gas Recovery:** Captures $H_2$ and $O_2$ off-gassing at charge and discharge terminals to recycle energy and improve round-trip efficiency.

## 1. Modular Core: The "Triple Crown" Discovery
This module links coal combustion residual recovery directly to data center infrastructure:

* **Primary Feedstock:** 600 TPD Coal Combustion Residual (CCR) / fly ash from power plants in the WV/OH industrial corridor.
* **Mineral Recovery:** Selective picolinic acid leaching yielding:
  * **99.9% Scandium Oxide**
  * **High-Purity Vanadium Pentoxide ($V_2O_5$)**
* **The "Electronic Blood" Fuel:** Recovered Vanadium feeds the Redox Flow Battery (VRFB) stacks for data center cooling and continuous backup power.
* **Infrastructure (Geocrete):** 6,000 PSI Geocrete binder formed from processed ash creates the structural "shell" for underground Utilidor piping.
