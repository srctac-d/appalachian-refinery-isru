# ⛏️ Appalachian ISRU: 600 TPD Coal Waste Refinery & Geocrete Synthesis
**Open Process Specification & Regional Material Recovery Model**  
*A modular, zero-waste refining framework converting Coal Ash and Acid Mine Drainage (AMD) sludge into battery-grade Vanadium, Scandium Oxide, and structural Geocrete.*

---

## 🏛️ Executive Overview

The Ohio Valley corridor holds vast stockpiles of post-industrial coal ash and acidic mine tailing waste[cite: 2]. Historically treated as environmental liabilities, these materials contain high concentrations of critical minerals necessary for energy independence, battery manufacturing, and resilient infrastructure[cite: 2, 3].

This specification details a **600 Ton-Per-Day (TPD) Refinery Model** designed to process coal ash and AMD sludge[cite: 2, 3]. Using a low-emission **Anaerobic Kiln (750°C)** and **Picolinic Acid selective leaching process**, the plant extracts high-purity rare earths and battery feedstocks while converting the remaining sterile silicate tailings into high-strength **6,000 PSI Geocrete**[cite: 2, 3].
