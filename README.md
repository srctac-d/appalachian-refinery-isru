# ⚡ EPCS: Electronic Blood (Coal-Waste-to-Data-Center)

> **License:** Open Hardware / Public Domain (Same as Core Project)  
> **Core Focus:** Circular refinery converting coal ash into battery-grade Vanadium, Scandium, and Geocrete Utilidor infrastructure for high-density AI data centers.

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary
The Electrochemical Power and Cooling System (EPCS) unifies raw material recovery with data center energy infrastructure. By extracting high-purity Vanadium Pentoxide ($V_2O_5$) from 600 TPD of Coal Combustion Residuals (CCR), EPCS fuels a closed-loop "Electronic Blood" Vanadium Redox Flow Battery (VRFB) system that supplies direct DC power and liquid thermal management to high-density server racks.

## 🌟 Primary System Features
1. **Critical Minerals & REE Recovery:** Addresses national supply chain security by extracting Rare Earth Elements and battery-grade metals ($V_2O_5$, Scandium) from coal combustion waste.
2. **Alkali Geocrete Conversion:** Replaces hazardous surface coal ash ponds by processing residual ash with alkaline activation to yield 6,000 PSI structural Geocrete.
3. **Electrochemical Voltage Translation:** Supplying direct DC power and liquid cooling to data centers via VRFB "Electronic Blood" running through underground Geocrete Utilidors.
## 🌟 Primary System Features for power plants
1. **Electrochemical Voltage Translation:** Replaces traditional wired copper transformers with stacked redox cells supplying direct DC power to cabinets.
2. **Dual-Function Core:** Electrolyte acts simultaneously as the energy storage medium and primary coolant, eliminating standalone HVAC systems.
3. **Circular Infrastructure:** Utilizes solid coal ash waste to create 6,000 PSI Geocrete shells for underground Utilidor fluid distribution.
